Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how the MoneyGlance Android app handles your information and the choices you have over your data.

1. Information stored on your phone

MoneyGlance stores your financial records and preferences locally on your device. This can include:

  • Transactions, amounts, dates, notes, accounts and categories.
  • Starting balances, transfers, refunds and recurring-entry schedules.
  • Your optional profile name and country, and app settings.

You can edit or clear your optional profile information. Selecting a country does not change your ledger currency.

2. Network access and third-party services

This version of MoneyGlance does not request internet access and has no advertising, analytics, account login or bank connection. It does not automatically upload your financial records to us.

Android and services you choose, such as an email app, file provider or sharing service, may use their own network connections and handle information under their own privacy policies.

3. Permissions and reminders

Notifications are optional and used for transaction reminders. Reminders and recurring-entry work may resume after your phone restarts and may be delayed by Android.

MoneyGlance does not request location access or broad file-storage access. You select individual backup or export files through Android's file picker.

4. App Lock and visibility controls

Optional App Lock uses Android's biometric or device-credential authentication. MoneyGlance receives an authentication result, not your phone password, PIN or biometric data. When enabled, it protects screenshots and recent-app previews and locks after a cold start or 30 seconds away.

App Lock does not encrypt the app's local database. Hide amounts masks summaries, lists and charts; explicit entry and edit screens still show exact values. Account inclusion in the main balance is a display preference, not a privacy lock.

5. Backups and exports

Manual backups contain your real financial records, optional profile and backed-up settings, even when amounts are hidden. Backup files are encrypted with the password you choose. MoneyGlance does not save that password and cannot recover it.

Backups do not contain your phone's App Lock, PIN, biometrics or notification permission. Restoring a backup replaces current records and backed-up settings after your confirmation.

You choose where to save or share files. A selected cloud storage or sharing provider may send or store them online. MoneyGlance does not upload backups automatically.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Local app information remains on your device until you edit or delete it, replace it through a confirmed restore, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Your optional name and country can be cleared in Profile.

Backup and export files saved elsewhere remain there until you delete them separately. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. Device manufacturers and services you choose may offer their own device-transfer behavior.

7. Support and bug reports

A bug report shared from MoneyGlance contains the text you review and basic app and Android version information. Financial records are not attached automatically. You choose the app or service used to share the report.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so that we can respond to your inquiry. Please avoid sending passwords or private financial records. Contact us using the address below for questions about support correspondence.

8. This policy webpage

This webpage explains the Android app's practices. GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when serving this webpage. This hosting information is separate from the financial records stored inside MoneyGlance.

For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when MoneyGlance's features or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Changes to data practices will be reflected in the app and its disclosures as appropriate.
